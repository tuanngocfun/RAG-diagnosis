A 19-year-old man from Syria (arrived in Denmark in 2015) presented in June 
2016 with a 6-month history of throat irritation that progressed over several days 
to severe throat pain and fever. He had no history of severe illness or excess 
infections; examination noted a scar on his arm that was suggestive of previous 
cutaneous leishmaniasis. Laryngoscopy demonstrated massive edema of the 
mucosa of both the pharynx and larynx; the epiglottis and uvula were swollen 
and red with papilloma-like lesions. Endoscopic images show marked swelling 
and papillomatous-appearing mucosal lesions of the oropharynx and larynx.
